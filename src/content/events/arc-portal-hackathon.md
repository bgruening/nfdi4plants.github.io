## Information

**ARC Portal Hackathon**  
*October 12th to 14th, 2026*

As part of the DataPLANT Community Call, we are excited to bring the community together for an ARC-Portal Hackathon, a collaborative event to shape the next generation of ARC access and discovery.

Together, we will explore and develop ways to:
  - Connect ARCs across DataHUBs by developing an ARC Portal that enables seamless access and discovery of ARCs from different DataHUBs.
  - Make ARCs easier to explore and understand by improving their presentation, visualization, and overall user experience, especially for researchers and users who are new to the ARC concept.

Location: Hotel Fettehenne, Erkrath

You can find our GitHub repository for the project here:
https://github.com/nfdi4plants/ARC-Portal

Let’s build, visualize, connect, and make ARCs more accessible together!
